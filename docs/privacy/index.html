Privacy

Updated 2026-11-19

Samepaste moves what you copy between your own devices. That content never comes to me or anyone else. This page lists everything that does leave your devices, and how to stop it.

What you copy

Copied text and pictures travel between your own devices and are never stored on a server I run. Copies an app marks as sensitive, such as a password manager’s, are not sent at all.

History on your devices

Each of your devices keeps its own history of what passed between them, so you can paste an older copy again: by default the last 7 days and at most 100 items (you can choose 1, 7 or 30 days and 50, 100 or 500 items). Older items are deleted on their own, and Settings › Clear now empties the history of that device. The history stays on your devices; it is never sent to me or stored on a server.

To make photos and screenshots searchable, each device reads the text in them itself (on the phone or tablet only while charging with the screen on; on the PC with Windows' own text recognition). That text stays on the device it was read on: it isn’t sent to your other devices or put into statistics or diagnostics. Copies marked as sensitive are not kept in the history.

App statistics, only if you say yes

The phone app and the PC program ask once whether to send usage statistics and crash reports, with two buttons of the same size and neither picked for you. Nothing is sent before you choose. You can change it in settings at any time; turning it off stops sending at once and drops anything not yet sent.

If you say yes, only the events below are sent. Every field is a fixed word or a range, never free text, and counts go once a day rather than per copy.

Never sent with statistics: what you copied, its exact length or size, text inside pictures, device names, your Google account or email address, phone numbers, precise location (GPS or street address), advertising ID, error message text. Crash reports carry only where the crash happened and its kind. Google estimates roughly the country and region from the connection’s IP address but does not store the IP; Google Analytics' granular location and device data collection is off in every region.

Recorded automatically by Firebase

Android only, and only after you chose to send statistics (before you are asked, or if you say no, Firebase collection is off). These records are added by the Firebase Analytics SDK itself, not chosen by our code, and never contain what you copied. Automatic screen-name logging (screen_view) is turned off.

Advertising features, Google signals and ad personalisation are off and ad data is redacted. The _ga cookie, holding a random ID, is set only while statistics are allowed. Where the site asks first, it loads nothing from Google until you allow it.

Who receives what

  • Google (Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, Google Analytics 4): the app’s, the PC program’s and this site’s statistics and crash reports above, in one place.
  • Google Play: your purchase when you buy on a phone. Google handles the payment.
  • Paddle: your purchase when you buy on a PC. Paddle is the seller of record and handles payment, tax and receipts.
  • RevenueCat: a record of what you bought, filed under a value made from your Google account instead of the account itself, so Pro works on every device signed in with that account.
  • Tailscale Inc., the connection service inside the app: when a device signs in, your Google account email, the device’s name and its IP address, so your devices can find each other. Never what you copy: devices connect directly and encrypted, and when a direct link isn’t possible the traffic passes through Tailscale relays still encrypted, so they cannot read it.
  • GitHub: hosts this website and the downloads. Like any web server it receives your IP address and browser details when you open a page or download a file, under GitHub’s own privacy statement. The fonts on this site are served from it too, so no font service sees your visit.
  • Google (Google Forms): your email address, if you leave it for the beta perk. It’s used only to send you the perk code once.

How long it’s kept

Statistics are kept for 14 months, then deleted. Purchase records stay with Google Play, Paddle and RevenueCat for as long as the purchase and the law require.

If you emailed to be told when Samepaste is out, your address is used for that one reply only, and I delete those emails once I’ve replied.

If you left your email for the beta perk, I delete it once the code has been sent.

How to turn it off

  • App and PC program: the statistics switch in settings.
  • This website: the button at the bottom of every page. The choice is kept in this browser, and after you turn statistics off no statistics requests are sent.
  • In the EU and EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and whenever the browser doesn’t tell which region it is in, the site asks first and loads nothing from Google Analytics until you allow it.
