Delete your Samepaste data

Updated 2026-11-19

This page is for Samepaste, the clipboard app by philaxis for Android phones and tablets (on Google Play) and Windows PCs. It shows how to delete the data the app creates, and what is kept afterwards.

Samepaste has no account of its own: no sign-up, no password, and nothing you copy is stored on a server. When you sign in with Google, the connection service inside the app (Tailscale) creates an account so your devices can find each other. Step 3 deletes it.

Steps to delete

  1. Clear the history and sign out on each device

    In Samepaste, open Settings, tap Clear now and confirm with Clear. That deletes the history on this device, including text read from pictures. Then sign out under Google account in Settings.

  2. Uninstall the app

    Uninstall Samepaste from every phone, tablet and PC. Everything it kept on the device goes with it, settings included.

  3. Delete the connection account

    Open the Tailscale admin console and sign in with the same Google account. Choose Delete tailnet and confirm with Delete. Tailscale Inc. then deletes your device list, the device names and your email address. If you sign in to Samepaste with that account again later, a new account is created.

  4. Ask me to delete the rest

    Email philaxis.dev@gmail.com from any address and tell me which Google account you signed in with. You don’t need the app for this. I delete your purchase record and the emails you sent me, then reply.

Use Tailscale yourself with the same Google account? Delete tailnet would remove your own network too. Instead, open the Machines page and remove only the devices that run Samepaste.

What is deleted

  • History on your devices and text read from pictures: deleted from that device as soon as you clear it or uninstall the app.
  • Device names, email address and IP addresses held by Tailscale Inc.: deleted from its servers when you delete the tailnet. Tailscale says copies in its diagnostic logs and backups usually expire within 60 days.
  • Your purchase record at RevenueCat: deleted when you ask.
  • Emails you sent me, this request included: deleted once I’ve replied. An address left for the beta perk is deleted once the code has been sent.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Usage statistics and crash reports, only if you chose to send them: they carry a random ID, never your name, email address or Google account, so one person’s can’t be picked out and deleted. They are deleted automatically after 14 months.
  • Purchase and tax records at Google Play and Paddle: kept by them for as long as the law requires.
  • What you copied: never stored on a server, so there is nothing of it to delete there.

More

Everything that leaves your devices, and who receives it, is in the privacy policy. Questions: philaxis.dev@gmail.com
