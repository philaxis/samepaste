One phone screenshot to the PC: seven ways, taps compared

Written by philaxis

Putting one phone screenshot into ChatGPT on the PC takes seven taps by email, six with Quick Share and one with Samepaste. Here are seven common ways, counted by each company’s own help, in one table.

Taps for one screenshot

WayTapsStepsWhat it needsQuality
Samepaste1Ctrl+V on the PCAndroid 11+ phone, Windows 10 or 11 PC, same Google accountOriginal
Phone Link, cross-device copy3Open screenshot › copy image › Ctrl+V on the PCSupported phones only (Samsung on One UI 2.1+, select HONOR, OPPO, ASUS, vivo, Xiaomi), Windows 10 or 11Over 1 MB resized
Samsung clipboard sharing3Open screenshot › copy image › Ctrl+V on the PCGalaxy phone, Windows 11 x64 PC with Galaxy Connect, same Samsung account and Wi‑Fi, BluetoothNot stated
File Explorer (Windows 11)5File Explorer › phone › Screenshots › find it › dragWindows 11, Android 11+, Link to WindowsOriginal
Google Photos5 + backup waitphotos.google.com › select › More options › Download › dragBackup on, Screenshots folder backed upFollows backup quality
Quick Share6Open screenshot › Share › Quick Share › your PC › open Downloads › drag64-bit Windows 10+, Bluetooth, same network, within 5 mNot stated by Google
Email it to yourself7Open › Share › mail app › your address › Send › open email › drag attachmentAny phone and PCDepends on the mail app

At a rough 2 seconds a tap, 10 screenshots a day costs about 100 seconds more with Quick Share and 120 more by email; over 20 days, about 33 and 40 minutes. If your phone has Phone Link’s copy and paste or Samsung clipboard sharing, the gap is about 4 seconds a screenshot.

Apps that move only text

Pushbullet and KDE Connect sync only text through the clipboard; KDE’s docs say “This does not work with images though.” (KDE). A picture goes as a file, about six taps like Quick Share.
