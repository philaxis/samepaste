Phone Link copy and paste not working? First check that your phone is supported

Android, WindowsUpdated 2026-11-20

Phone Link shows your messages and photos on the PC, but what you copy on the phone won’t paste on the PC, or the other way round. Often nothing is broken: copy and paste is the one part of Phone Link that only some phones get. Microsoft’s help says so at the top of the section:

“This is applicable for Surface Duo and select Samsung and HONOR and OPPO and ASUS and vivo and Xiaomi devices.”

Microsoft Support, Phone Link

Microsoft’s own fixes, in order

  1. Check that your phone is on the list

    Microsoft lists Surface Duo, Samsung phones running One UI 2.1 or later, and select HONOR, OPPO (with OnePlus and Realme), ASUS, vivo and Xiaomi models. On a Samsung, Settings › About › Software information shows the One UI version. If your phone isn’t on the list, Microsoft doesn’t support copy and paste on it, and the next two steps won’t change that.

  2. Turn the feature on in Phone Link

    On the PC, open Phone Link and go to Settings › Features › Cross-device copy and paste. Make sure Allow this app to access and transfer content I copy and paste between my phone and PC is On.

  3. Close both apps and open them again

    Close Phone Link on the PC and Link to Windows on the phone, then open both. If your latest messages and photos show up on the PC, the connection works and copy and paste should too. As a last resort, restart the PC.

Also worth knowing

A long piece of text may not cross at all: Microsoft says it can exceed the allowed amount, so copy it in smaller parts. Only text and images cross, and images over 1 MB are resized.

Away from Wi‑Fi it works only if the phone may use mobile data. On a Samsung that’s Settings › Advanced features › Link to Windows › Use mobile data. If you use Microsoft Edge, Microsoft also suggests updating it. All of this is from Microsoft’s Phone Link help.

If your phone isn’t on the list

If it is, keep Phone Link: it’s already on your PC and also brings your messages, calls and notifications. Samepaste only does the clipboard.

If it isn’t, Samepaste does the copy and paste part on any Android 11 or later phone, Galaxy or not, with any Windows 10 or 11 PC. Copy on one, paste on the other, both ways. Photos and screenshots you take go across too, at full quality.

  1. Samepaste

    2 steps

    1. Copy on the phone
    2. Press Ctrl+V on the PC

It doesn’t need Phone Link or a Microsoft account: you sign in with the same Google account on both. The phone can be on mobile data while the PC is on Wi‑Fi. On my own devices, text copied on the PC reached the phone in 0.1 to 0.3 seconds.

Two steps is with the Android app from this site. On the Google Play version, text copied on the phone needs one more tap: Send now on its quick settings tile.

What Samepaste needs

  • An Android 11 or later phone and a Windows 10 or 11 PC, signed in with the same Google account. No iPhone, iPad, Mac or Linux.
  • Text you copy in another app: the Android app from this site notices it by itself about a second after you lift your finger. On some phones that can be late or miss one; Copy detection in Samepaste’s settings catches each copy as it happens. The Google Play version can’t read copies from other apps by itself: copy, then tap Send now on its quick settings tile.
  • Copies an app marks as sensitive, like a password manager’s, stay on the device they were copied on.
  • Free during the open beta, on up to 10 devices.
