How it goes

  1. Copy the address on the PC

    Select it anywhere and press Ctrl+C.

  2. It’s on the phone’s clipboard

    Nothing to tap on the phone. On my own devices it took 0.1 to 0.3 seconds.

  3. Paste it in the phone’s browser

    Long-press the address bar and choose Paste.

What you need

  • PC and phone signed in with the same Google account
  • Any network: the phone can be on mobile data while the PC is on home Wi‑Fi

It’s copy and paste: Samepaste doesn’t open links on the other device by itself.
