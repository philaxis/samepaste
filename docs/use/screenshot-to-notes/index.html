SamepasteUses

A screenshot, straight into a note on the same phone

A map, a booking screen, a Wi-Fi password. Screenshot what you want to keep, then just paste it into your notes.

Android
Maps
Notes

Saturday show

How it goes

  1. Take a screenshot

    The way you always do. Camera photos work too.

  2. It’s on this phone’s clipboard

    No send button, no gallery. It’s there at full quality.

  3. Paste it into a note

    In a notes app that takes pictures, long-press and choose Paste.

What you need

  • One Android 11+ phone, signed in with Google. No PC needed
  • Screenshots (and Camera photos) on in Samepaste’s settings

Works even with sending to other devices off. Only the camera and screenshot folders count.
