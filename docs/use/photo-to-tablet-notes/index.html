A photo from your phone, straight into the notes on your tablet

The tablet is busy being your notebook and the phone is the better camera. Use both.

Android phone
Camera
Android tablet
Notes

Lecture 4

How it goes

  1. Photograph the board with the phone

    Leave the tablet where it is and use the phone’s camera.

  2. It’s on the tablet’s clipboard

    Full quality, no share menu on either device.

  3. Paste it into the note

    Long-press where it goes, choose Paste, keep writing.

What you need

  • Phone and tablet signed in with the same Google account
  • Camera photos (and Screenshots) on in Samepaste’s settings on the phone

A phone and a tablet are two devices. During the open beta everything works on up to 10 devices; after launch, two are free.
